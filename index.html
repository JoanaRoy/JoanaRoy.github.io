About Me

Personal introduction & professional aspirations

Hello! I’m Joana Rose D. Roy, an Information Technology student at North Eastern Mindanao State University in Cantilan, Surigao del Sur. I’m pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology, with an expected graduation in 2027.

I’m passionate about exploring technology and learning how thoughtful digital solutions are made. My interests include web development, UI/UX design, software development, and database management. I enjoy combining creativity with problem-solving and discovering new tools along the way.

My goal is to become a skilled IT professional who contributes to meaningful projects. I’m motivated to keep learning, strengthen my technical skills, and gain practical experience while working and growing with others.
